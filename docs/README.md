# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation! This resource centralizes our project management processes, workflows, roles, and best practices to help teams deliver high-quality products efficiently.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes customer-first thinking, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. Our process is built on principles of psychological safety, transparent communication, and continuous improvement. Each project progresses through a defined lifecycle—from initiation and planning through execution, release, and retrospective—with clear roles and responsibilities at every stage.

The project management framework at OctoAcme ensures that cross-functional teams can collaborate effectively by providing standardized workflows, communication cadences, and quality gates. Project Managers coordinate delivery and manage risks, Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work, and Developers implement features while maintaining high quality standards. Throughout the project lifecycle, we maintain key artifacts such as project charters, roadmaps, risk registers, and retrospective notes to ensure alignment and institutional knowledge.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout our processes, from acceptance criteria definition during planning to automated testing in CI/CD pipelines and manual QA validation before release. We emphasize small, testable increments and maintain clear Definition of Done criteria. Communication strategies include regular standups, weekly syncs between PMs and Product Managers, monthly stakeholder updates, and structured escalation paths to ensure transparency and rapid issue resolution.

This documentation is maintained as part of our Copilot Space to scale institutional knowledge across the organization. By centralizing, refining, and sharing process knowledge in this repository, we enable GitHub Copilot to provide context-aware guidance to team members, answer questions about our workflows, and help new teammates onboard quickly. The structured documentation in this space serves as a single source of truth for how OctoAcme runs projects.
